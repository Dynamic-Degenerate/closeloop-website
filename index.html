FAQ

Questions, answered

What states does Closeloop support?

Closeloop ships with contract deadline data for major real estate markets and a sensible default for any other state, with more states added regularly.

How accurate is the AI contract scanning?

Closeloop uses Claude, Anthropic's AI model, to extract dates and terms from your uploaded contracts. Always double-check extracted dates against your signed contract before relying on them.

Can my clients see their own portal?

Yes — every buyer and seller gets a private, branded link to track their transaction's progress in real time.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, new accounts start with a free trial of the Pro plan — no credit card required to get started.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Closeloop is billed monthly with no long-term contract, and you can cancel from your account settings at any time.
